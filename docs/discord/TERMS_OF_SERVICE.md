## What the application does

The application connects your running FINAL FANTASY XIV 1.x client to your
Discord profile through Discord's local Rich Presence interface. While you play,
Discord may display a status such as "Playing Final Fantasy XIV 1.X" on your
profile. The application does not use voice, video, guild, messaging, or any
other Discord API feature.
